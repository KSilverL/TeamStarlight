# Twitter / X — platform style skill

The creator reads this file and injects it as the static style layer when drafting an
X post (this skill also serves the `x` handle); the roundtable's `platform_editor` seat
reads the same file. Edit it to retune the house style.

## Format
- Character limit: 280
- One sharp idea per post. If it genuinely needs more, it's a thread — open with a 🧵 and
  make tweet 1 stand fully on its own.
- The first ~5 words are the whole game; front-load the payoff, cut every spare word.
- 0–2 hashtags, woven into a sentence, never a wall at the end.
- 1 emoji max, as punctuation — not decoration. No "link in bio" (that's Instagram).

## Anatomy of a strong post
1. **Payoff first**: the surprising claim, number, or opinion — in the opening clause.
2. **The turn**: one line of why it's true or why it matters.
3. **(Thread only)**: tweet 1 promises the payload ("here's why 👇"); each reply is one
   self-contained beat, no "as I said above".

## Tone
- Punchy, emotional, brevity-first. Front-load the payoff; cut every spare word.
- A strong hook in the first 5 words. Opinion > announcement.
- Conversational — write like a smart person talking, not a brand.

## Openers that work
- "Hot take: [claim most people would push back on]."
- "[Surprising number]. Here's why that changes everything 👇"
- "Nobody tells you this about [topic]:"

## Calls to action that work
- End on the idea itself, or "Reply with your take 👇" · "What would you add?"
- Skip "RT if you agree" and hashtag-driven CTAs — the strong take is the CTA.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ One idea; payoff in the first clause; conversational; under 280.
- ✅ A thread only when the idea truly needs it, each tweet self-contained.
- ❌ Corporate voice, hashtag dumps, burying the point, emoji clutter, running past 280.

## Good example
> Hot take: most cold brew is over-extracted battery acid. 🧵
>
> Ours steeps 16 hours at 4°C. Here's why that one number changes everything 👇

## Bad example (avoid)
> We are pleased to inform our valued customers that our brand-new cold brew product
> offering is now officially available for purchase across all of our retail channels
> #coffee #coldbrew #summer #drink #yum #sale #new #buy

Why it's bad: corporate voice, no hook, runs past 280, hashtag dump, zero emotion.

## Self-check before posting
- Is the payoff in the first 5 words?
- Under 280, ≤2 woven hashtags, ≤1 emoji, one idea only?
- If it's a thread, does tweet 1 stand alone and promise the rest?
