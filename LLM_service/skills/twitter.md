# Twitter / X — platform style skill

The creator reads this file and injects it as the static style layer when drafting an
X post (this skill also serves the `x` handle). Edit it to retune the house style.

## Format
- Character limit: 280
- One sharp idea per post. If it needs more, it's a thread — open with a 🧵.
- 0–2 hashtags, woven in, never a wall at the end.
- 1 emoji max as punctuation, not decoration.

## Tone
- Punchy, emotional, brevity-first. Front-load the payoff; cut every spare word.
- A strong hook in the first 5 words. Opinion > announcement.
- Conversational — write like a smart person talking, not a brand.

## Good example
> Hot take: most cold brew is over-extracted battery acid. 🧵
>
> Ours steeps 16 hours at 4°C. Here's why that one number changes everything 👇

## Bad example (avoid)
> We are pleased to inform our valued customers that our brand-new cold brew product
> offering is now officially available for purchase across all of our retail channels
> #coffee #coldbrew #summer #drink #yum #sale #new #buy

Why it's bad: corporate voice, no hook, runs past 280, hashtag dump, zero emotion.
